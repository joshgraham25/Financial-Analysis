# Two-Minute Tuesday

A weekly two-minute video showing one trick, shortcut, or better habit in the
software we use every day — the ERP, Outlook, Teams, Excel, Windows itself.

One tip. One video. Under two minutes. Every Tuesday.

## Why this name

Four candidates were on the table: *The Shortcut*, *Tech Tip Tuesday*,
*Two-Minute Tuesday*, and *Work Smarter Weekly*. **Two-Minute Tuesday** won for
three reasons:

1. **It answers the only real objection up front.** Nobody skips a video because
   they don't want to learn — they skip it because they don't know if it will
   cost them two minutes or fifteen. The name removes that question.
2. **It sets the cadence.** People learn when to expect it, which is what turns
   a series into a habit instead of a broadcast.
3. **It disciplines the producer.** The title is a promise with a number in it.
   You cannot ship a six-minute Two-Minute Tuesday, so scope creep dies at the
   scripting stage.

The tradeoff is real and worth naming: the weekday is now a public commitment,
and a skipped Tuesday is visible. Mitigation is in
[`production/checklist.md`](production/checklist.md) — record two episodes ahead
and keep one evergreen episode finished in the bank.

## The rules that keep it watchable

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Hard stop at 2:00, aim for 1:45 | The name is a contract. Over 2:00 and you have broken it. |
| One tip per episode | Two tips means neither gets remembered. Split it into two Tuesdays. |
| Open with the pain, not the feature | "Stop copy-pasting your vendor list" beats "Today we'll learn UNIQUE." |
| Real company data, never `Foo`/`Bar` | People adopt what they recognize. Use job numbers, cost centers, real vendors. |
| No intro music, no logo animation | Eight seconds of branding on a 120-second video is 7% of the runtime. |
| Show the finished result in the first 15 seconds | Viewers stay for a payoff they can already see. |
| End on a screenshot-able recap frame | The recap card is the thing people actually keep. |

## Getting the first episode out

1. Read [`production/recording-sop.md`](production/recording-sop.md) once, start to finish.
2. Open [`episodes/001-excel-dynamic-arrays/script.md`](episodes/001-excel-dynamic-arrays/script.md)
   and [`episodes/001-excel-dynamic-arrays/demo-workbook.xlsx`](episodes/001-excel-dynamic-arrays/demo-workbook.xlsx).
3. Do one full rehearsal take without recording. Time it. Cut words until it lands under 1:50.
4. Record, edit, publish per [`production/publishing.md`](production/publishing.md).

Expect the first episode to take about 90 minutes end to end. By episode four
it should be 25 minutes, because the template, the title card, and the Clipchamp
project are all reused.

## Roles

Keep this to one owner and one reviewer. More people means slower Tuesdays.

- **Producer** — picks the tip from the backlog, scripts, records, edits, publishes.
- **Reviewer** — watches once before publish, checking only two things: is it
  accurate, and is it under two minutes.
