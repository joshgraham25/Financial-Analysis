# The first announcement — what Josh forwards ahead of episode 001

Sent once, by hand, forwarding a copy of the real email so people recognise it when it lands.
Not sent by the app: the app sends episodes, and this is a one-off introduction.

**Why it is written this way:** it does not lead with "this is not phishing", because phishing says
that too. It leads with who it is from and what it is, then hands the reader something they can
*verify themselves* — hover the button and the domain is CLC's own SharePoint. The "it will never
ask you to sign in" line is deliberately a rule rather than a reassurance: it stays true, it makes a
real phish stand out, and it survives someone spoofing the name later. "Report it" is there so this
does not train people to lower their guard.

---

## English

**Subject:** Two-Minute Training Tuesday — starting next Tuesday

> Hi all,
>
> Starting next Tuesday I'll be sending a short training video each week: one trick or shortcut in
> the software we use every day — Excel, Outlook, Teams, the ERP. One tip, under two minutes, every
> Tuesday.
>
> The email below is what it will look like, so you know it when it lands. It comes from my address
> and shows as **Two-Minute Training Tuesday**. The video lives in our own SharePoint under CLC
> Training — hover the button and you'll see `creativeliquidcoatingsfw.sharepoint.com`. There are no
> attachments, and it will never ask you to sign in, enter a password, or approve anything. If one
> ever does, it isn't from me — report it.
>
> First episode: getting a unique list in Excel without copying, pasting and running Remove
> Duplicates every month.
>
> If something in your week wastes time and you'd like it covered — ERP, Outlook, Teams, anything —
> just reply. Replies come to me and they're where the topic list comes from.
>
> Josh

## Spanish

Neutral Latin American Spanish, "ustedes". **The series name stays in English** on purpose — it is
the thing people learn to recognise in their inbox, and translating the brand splits it in two.

a draft and not fine for the first thing the whole company reads in Spanish.

## What is NOT in this message, deliberately

- **No mention of the pilot send to seven people.** Explaining that a few colleagues already got it
  invites questions that help nobody.
- **No apology for the extra email.** It is one message introducing a series people did not ask for;
  apologising for it invites them to agree it was unwelcome.
