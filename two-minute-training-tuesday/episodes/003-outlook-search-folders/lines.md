# Episode 003 — lines to read while recording

Read these in order, out loud, while you drive Outlook. **Pause about a second between
lines and don't pause mid-line** — that pause is the only signal the tooling has for
where one caption ends and the next begins.

Don't worry about the delivery. Your audio is a metronome and never ships; Andrew speaks
the final cut. Wording can drift — the captions get rewritten from what you actually
said, so say it however it comes out.

The last two lines are the outro. Say them, but they get cut from the picture and spoken
over the recap card instead.

---

Welcome to Two-Minute Training Tuesday.
This folder holds every training idea anyone sends me.
Every one of these got here because I moved it by hand.
So the ones I forgot to file aren't here at all.
A Search Folder fixes that. It's a folder that fills itself.
On the Folder tab — New Search Folder.
Scroll to the bottom. Create a custom Search Folder.
Choose. Name it Training ideas.
Criteria. The words two minute, in the subject or body.
Browse — the whole mailbox, including subfolders.
OK, OK, OK.
There it is, under Search Folders.
Every email that mentions it — including the ones I never filed.
Here's the part people miss.
That email is still sitting in my Inbox. Nothing moved.
A Search Folder is a saved search, not a place.
That's the difference from a rule. A rule moves mail. This just finds it.
So deleting from here deletes the real email. That's the one catch.
Unread across every folder. Anything with a big attachment. Everything from your boss.
That's it for this week.
