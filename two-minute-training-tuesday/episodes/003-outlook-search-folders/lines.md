# Episode 003 — lines to read while recording

**New Outlook / Outlook on the web.** Verified 2026-09-17 by building the folder for real and
deleting it again: the two render this flow identically, so either is safe to record.

Read these in order, out loud, while you drive Outlook. **Pause about a second between
lines and don't pause mid-line** — that pause is the only signal the tooling has for where
one caption ends and the next begins.

Don't worry about the delivery. Your audio is a metronome and never ships; Andrew speaks
the final cut.

**Read [`recording-setup.md`](recording-setup.md) first** — the six-toggle settings pass.

The last two lines are the outro: say them, but they get cut from the picture and spoken
over the recap card.

---

Welcome to Two-Minute Training Tuesday.
This folder holds every training idea anyone sends me.
Every one of these got here because I moved it by hand.
And the ones I never filed are scattered everywhere else.
A Search Folder fixes that. It's a folder that fills itself.
Settings — the gear, top right.
Mail, then Search folders.
Add search folder.
Name it first — it won't let you go on without one.
Two-Minute Training.
Then pick a type. There's a whole list of these.
Mail with specific words.
And the words: Two-Minute Training. Enter, and it becomes a tag.
On its own, though, it does not search everywhere.
Tick Filter to specific folders.
Add your Inbox, and leave Search subfolders ticked.
Then Sent Items. And Deleted Items, which it skips otherwise.
Save.
There it is, down with the other search folders.
Forty-four messages, from three different folders at once.
Here's the part people miss. Look at the tag on each one.
Deleted Items. Sent Items. My ideas folder. That's where they still live.
Nothing moved. A Search Folder is a saved search, not a place.
A rule moves your mail. This one just finds it.
So deleting a message from here deletes the real message. That's the one catch.
I've got a few of these now — one per project, one per person I trade a lot of mail with.
That's it for this week.
