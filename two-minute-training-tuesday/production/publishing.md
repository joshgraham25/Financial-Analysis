# Publishing

Two destinations, chosen for two different jobs:

- **Teams channel + its SharePoint library** — the weekly nudge, plus the
  permanent searchable back catalog. A file posted to a Teams channel lands in
  that channel's SharePoint document library automatically, so you get both from
  one upload.
- **Viva Engage** — reach beyond the people who follow the channel, and a place
  for questions and requests to accumulate.

Publish Tuesday morning, before lunch. Afternoon posts compete with the
afternoon.

---

## 1. Teams channel

### One-time setup

1. Create a dedicated channel — **Two-Minute Training Tuesday** — in whichever team
   everyone is already in. Do not make a new team for it; a new team is a new
   thing to remember to visit.
2. Pin a post to the channel explaining what the series is and when it lands.
3. In the channel's **Files** tab, create folders per app: `Excel/`, `Outlook/`,
   `Teams/`, `ERP/`, `Windows/`. This is the SharePoint library, and this is what
   makes the back catalog browsable a year from now.

### Every week

1. **Files** tab → the right app folder → **Upload** the MP4.
2. Start a new channel post. Attach or link the video so it plays inline.
3. Paste the episode's copy from its `post-copy.md`.
4. Post the **written summary in the message body**, not only in the video.
   Search indexes your text; it does not index your narration.
5. Attach the demo workbook if people can reuse it.

Post as a new conversation each week, never a reply to last week's. Replies bury.

### Keep the library usable

In the SharePoint library, add these columns once and fill them in on upload:

| Column | Type | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Episode | Number | 1 |
| App | Choice | Excel |
| Tip | Text | UNIQUE, SORT, FILTER |
| Published | Date | 2026-08-25 |

Thirty episodes in, this is the difference between a library and a junk drawer.
Someone will search "vendor list" in October and needs to land on episode 1.

---

## 2. Viva Engage

1. Post to the all-company community, or a **Two-Minute Training Tuesday** community if
   volume justifies one later.
2. Upload the same MP4 natively rather than linking out. Native video plays in
   the feed; a link asks for a click most people won't spend.
3. Use the shorter Viva copy from `post-copy.md` — the feed rewards brevity.
4. **Answer every comment.** The comments are your backlog: "can you do this for
   the ERP?" is next month's episode, handed to you for free.

---

## 3. Optional: pin the current episode in Outlook

If adoption is slow after a month, add a one-line link to the current episode in
the company email signature or an existing internal newsletter. Do not start a
dedicated weekly email — video in email gets poor click-through and it burns
goodwill faster than it earns viewers.

---

## Measuring whether this is working

Check monthly, not weekly. Weekly numbers on internal video are noise.

| Signal | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Views in the first 48 hours | Whether the topic hit a real annoyance |
| Comments and questions | Engagement, and free backlog |
| Requests for a specific tip | The series has become a thing people expect |
| People citing an episode number in unrelated chats | Success. This is the real metric. |

If views sag for three weeks running, the problem is almost always topic
selection, not production quality. Ask the loudest complainer on your team what
wastes their time, and make that.
